About

WR Saunders is an artist and filmmaker working in contemporary art and narrative cinema. His art practice encompasses experimental and essay films, installations and other works, often engaging with overlooked objects, accumulated material and footage originally made for other purposes.

Alongside this, he writes and directs fiction films and music videos, and works with other artists and filmmakers in camera, editing and documentary roles. These different strands of work are presented separately under Art, Film and Credits.
