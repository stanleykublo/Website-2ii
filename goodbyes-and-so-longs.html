Goodbyes and So Longs

2020 · Installation

An installation of impressions hand-pressed into fragile coloured foil and pinned to a wall. The pieces rustle in the updraught of a domestic bar heater, as though trying to free themselves.

A meditation on the accumulation of identity through experience, repetition and education, and its dissolution through forgetting, impermanence and death.
